# Add three process tracing references to Zotero

Written 5 September 2026. Small but time-limited: **the next Zotero auto-export overwrites `MyLibrary.bib` and these three go with it**, and the theory-fit working paper then renders three citations as "n.d.".

They were appended by hand to `Causal Map\MyLibrary.bib` on 5 September so the draft renders now. Backup of the previous file is beside it as `MyLibrary.bib.bak-20260905`, safe to delete once these are in Zotero.

The three, all verified against primary sources during the literature search:

- **Fairfield, T. and Charman, A. E. (2017)**, "Explicit Bayesian Analysis for Process Tracing: Guidelines, Opportunities, and Caveats", *Political Analysis* 25(3): 363-380. DOI `10.1017/pan.2017.14`. Key `fairfieldExplicitBayesianAnalysis2017`.
- **Befani, B. and Mayne, J. (2014)**, "Process Tracing and Contribution Analysis: A Combined Approach to Generative Causal Inference for Impact Evaluation", *IDS Bulletin* 45(6): 17-36. DOI `10.1111/1759-5436.12110`. Key `befaniProcessTracingContribution2014`.
- **Beach, D., Camacho, G. and Siewert, M. (2022)**, "Going beyond the single case: Comparative Process Tracing as a tool to enable generalizations about causal processes", APSA Annual Meeting, Montreal, on APSA Preprints. Key `beachGoingBeyondSingle2022`. Conference preprint, and no peer-reviewed version was found. Worth asking the authors at EES, since two of the three run PDW 19 on 27 October.


Beach and Pedersen (2019) was already in the library at line 1070 under `beachProcessTracingMethods2019`, same DOI, so it needs nothing. Worth adding `edition = {2nd}` to it in Zotero when you are there, since the second edition has four variants of process tracing and the first had three.
